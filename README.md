# Health-Data-Analytics

# PROBLEM STATEMENT 

Economic indicators ,particulary income levels significantly dictate the public health outcomes of both developed(high income) and developing countries(low income).Over time,many developing countries have experienced an economic growth which has remodelled their healthcare sector.The rise in gdp has  increased the counties governemtn capacity to invest in health expenditure and therefore cater for healthcare infrastucture which includes hospital beds,physicians and nurses.In developed countries ,sustainable economic growth has seen to continuous health expenfiture in halthcare infrastructure and services thereby contributing to high life expectancy by reducing mortality rate due to a great vaccine coverage  and prenatal and post natal services sanitation.Contrastingly ,the dveloping and underdeveloped countries have been on the knife edge where they have reduced gdp and therefore health expenditure resulting in inadeauate health infrastructure and services this has had an adverse effects on the survival rates and therefore the population growth.Understanding the relationship between economic growth and health is important for generating public health insights whihc aims at improving health outcomes in both countries.



## Features
 
 * ** Health indicators  :** has the following data.

 Demographics: population, population growth, urban pct, fertility rate adolescent fertility.

 Health Outcomes: life expectancy, death and birth rate, under5,neonatal and maternal mortalities .

 Disease & Risk Factors: tb and hiv incidences, obesity pct, smoking male and female, alcohol per capita, diabetes pct.

 Infrastructure & Care: health_expenditure_pct_gdp, physicians,hospital beds and nurses per_1000, immunization, prenatal care pct.

 Economy & Poverty: gdp per capita, gdp_per_capita_ppp, poverty rate

 * ** Country latest :**  has Identification: Country_code, country_name, region, income_level, year

 * ** Indicator_metadata :** has indicator code,name,category,unit and description

 * ** data frame : ** store the data above

 * ** plot : ** to visualise the different analysis done
