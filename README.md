# Health-Data-Analytics

# PROBLEM STATEMENT 

Developing countries have experienced rapid urbanisation and economic growth which has remodelled their health sectors over the years.While urbanisation has contributed to an increase in economic growth it has led to the epidemic transition from infectious/communicable to non-communicable/lifestyle related diseases caused by behaviours like smoking among different population groups.Consequently high gdp due to the economic growth has resulted in an increase in  health expenditure,enabling investments in healthcare infrastucture  like hospital beds,physicians and nurses.This is expected to lower infant mortality rates through region wide vaciine courage to infants and maternal mortality rate due to care before and after delivery,but its impact on the population growth is unclear.Comprehending the trio relationship between urbanisation,economic growth and health is vital in giving public health insights to improve the health outcomes in these countries.


## Features
 
 * ** Health indicators  :** has the following data.

 Demographics: population, population growth, urban pct, fertility rate adolescent fertility.

 Health Outcomes: life expectancy, death and birth rate, under5,neonatal and maternal mortalities .

 Disease & Risk Factors: tb and hiv incidences, obesity pct, smoking male and female, alcohol per capita, diabetes pct.

 Infrastructure & Care: health_expenditure_pct_gdp, physicians,hospital beds and nurses per_1000, immunization, prenatal care pct.

 Economy & Poverty: gdp per capita, gdp_per_capita_ppp, poverty rate

 * ** Country latest :**  has Identification: Country_code, country_name, region, income_level, year

 * ** Indicator_metadata :** has indicator code,name,category,unit and description

 * ** data frame : ** store the data above

 * ** plot : ** to visualise the different analysis done
